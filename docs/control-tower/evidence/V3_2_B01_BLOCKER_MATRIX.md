## Exact external gates

1. **A08:** independent child-safety reviewer must review the B01 hazard evidence, per-step adult-assist/safety classification, and EN/PT safety copy; return an explicit decision and required changes.
2. **A09:** independent native PT-BR and child-experience reviewer must review B01 ingredient/step translations and the safety drafts for accuracy, clarity and age suitability; return explicit approval or corrections.
3. **Nutrition/allergens:** provide traceable, authoritative nutrition inputs and source-backed calculation/serving basis for each recipe; provide ingredient/product allergen evidence (including relevant substitutions); obtain independent review. No values or allergen declarations were inferred here.
4. **Artwork rights:** provide original-asset identity/provenance and written rights/usage approval for each asset used, or an approved replacement asset and rights record. Hashes in this inventory are not rights evidence.
5. **Signing:** signing authority must approve the exact B01 APK signing identity/key and distribution type; CI must verify package/signature without changing Classic or user data.
