# 101 — Cucumber Sesame Salad / Salada de pepino com gergelim

Status: READY_FOR_INTEGRATION (content/artwork/schema completeness only; independent reviews pending) · serves 4 · ages 8–10

EN-GB: Cool cucumber tossed with a tangy soy, rice vinegar and sesame dressing.

PT-BR: Pepino fresco misturado com molho agridoce de soja, vinagre de arroz e gergelim.

## Ingredients
- 400 g — Cucumber / Pepino
- 30 mL — Unseasoned rice vinegar / Vinagre de arroz sem tempero
- 15 mL — Reduced-sodium soy sauce / Molho de soja com menos sódio
- 10 mL — Sesame oil / Óleo de gergelim
- 4 g — Caster sugar / Açúcar refinado
- 5 g — Sesame seeds / Sementes de gergelim
- 1 clove — Garlic clove / Dente de alho
- 10 g — Spring onion / Cebolinha

## Equipment
- Chopping board / Tábua de corte — adult
- Kitchen knife / Faca de cozinha — adult
- Small bowl / Tigela pequena — shared
- Mixing bowl / Tigela para misturar — shared
- Mixing spoon / Colher para misturar — shared
- Measuring spoon / Colher medidora — shared
- Serving bowl / Tigela para servir — shared

## Steps

### step-01 — Wash the vegetables / Lave os vegetais
- EN-GB: Wash the cucumber and spring onion under clean running water.
- PT-BR: Lave o pepino e a cebolinha em água corrente limpa.
- Adult help EN-GB: Adults handle the sharp knife. Children wash vegetables, measure dressing and toss the salad with supervision.
- Ajuda PT-BR: Adultos usam a faca afiada. Crianças lavam os vegetais, medem o molho e misturam a salada com supervisão.
- Safety EN-GB: Wash hands and produce. An adult uses the sharp knife on a stable board. Check soy sauce and sesame labels for allergens and cross-contact; refrigerate salad and leftovers promptly.
